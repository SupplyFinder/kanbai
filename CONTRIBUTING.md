# Contributing

Thanks for helping. Issues and pull requests are welcome.

- **Bugs**: open an issue with what you did, what you expected, what happened, and the board's Health
  panel text if relevant. Never paste tokens, keys or customer data.
- **Changes to the skill** (`.claude/skills/kanbai/`): keep it generic; anything company-specific belongs in
  the company files (`kanbai/`). Bump the version line in `SKILL.md` and add a line to `CHANGELOG.md`.
- **Changes to the board** (`board/board.html`): one self-contained page; test light and dark themes and a
  phone-width view.
- Write plainly: the board and the skill are read by non-developers too.
